Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

Coder (coder.webart.work and its Android and iOS apps, package work.webart.coder) is a software architecture learning platform. We collect as little as we can: every lesson and exercise works without an account, we show no ads, we do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

Who is responsible

Coder is operated by WebArt Work. For any question about this policy or your data, write to {{ email }}.

What we collect

Without signing in

Coder signs you in as an anonymous guest so your results can be saved. This creates a random guest identifier. Your answers are checked in your browser or app, and only your best result for each lesson is stored under that identifier. No name, email or other personal details are collected.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, we store:

  • the name, email address and profile photo link of your Google account;
  • your best result for each lesson;
  • the systems you build from lessons, if you save them.

On your device

Your chosen language and theme are kept in your browser or app storage on your device. Clearing the site data or the app data removes them.

Technical data

Like any website, the hosting provider and Google services may process technical request data such as your IP address, device and browser type to deliver the service and keep it secure.

How we use it

Only to run Coder: to sign you in, show your profile and keep your progress between visits and devices. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or analytics. Our database rules let each account read and write only its own data.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the services that run the app on our behalf, and only as needed for that:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) handles sign-in and stores the account data above. Firebase privacy
  • Google Sign-In processes your Google account when you choose to sign in with it. Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and, like any web server, logs technical data such as your IP address. GitHub privacy statement

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We may disclose data only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep account data until you delete your account. Guest data is kept until you delete it or stops being used.

Delete your account and data

Open your profile on the website or in the app and choose Delete account. You may be asked to sign in once more, then your account and everything stored for it are deleted right away.

If you can no longer sign in, email {{ email }} from the address of your account and we will delete the data for you.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email {{ email }} and we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Coder is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Signing in with Google follows Google's own age rules. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to {{ email }} and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
